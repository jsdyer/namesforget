What’s in the index

Natural Resources Canada publishes the current officially approved geographical names in weekly CSV extracts. This index combines the national and undersea files and deduplicates records by CGNDB key.

A name or designation says nothing about the current condition, historical use, or accessibility of a place. Some historical naming terms in the government source may be offensive. The decision date describes the name, not when the feature came into use.
